Postmark

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version

Postmark is a travel passport app. We only collect what the app needs to work: your stamps, your trips, the photos you choose to add, and, if you create an account, your email address. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you create an account, your email address and a password (stored securely by our sign-in provider). You can use Postmark as a guest without an account.
  • Your passport. The cities you stamp and when, your trips, packing lists, postcards, quiz progress, avatar and settings.
  • Location, only when you ask. When you tap “Stamp here”, the app checks your location once to confirm you’re in that city. If you turn on the “near me” setting, it uses your location to show nearby cities. We store the result of the check (that the stamp was verified, with its accuracy), not a history of where you’ve been.
  • Photos you choose. Only photos you pick yourself. The app reads each photo’s date and location to file it under the right city, and saves a resized copy.
  • Friends. If you add friends, we store who you’re friends with, the postcards you send each other and trips you plan together.

Where it’s kept

  • On your device by default, including your photos.
  • In your account, if you sign in, so your passport and photos are backed up and work on your other devices. Account data is stored with our database and storage provider, Supabase. Photos are kept in a private folder that only your account can open.
  • The website is hosted by Vercel, and the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your device’s IP address when you open the app.

Who can see what

  • Friends you add can see your first name, home city, avatar, the cities you’ve stamped and your stamp photos. Photos you mark as private are never shared.
  • People on a trip you plan together can see the trip and the trip photos you’ve shared with it.
  • Postcards are only visible to the person you send them to.
  • Nobody else can see your passport, and we never sell or rent your information.

Your choices

  • Turn location off at any time in the app or in your phone’s settings. You can still add past visits as memory stamps.
  • Make any photo private, or delete it, from its photo view.
  • Delete your account: Passport → Account → “Delete my account”. This permanently removes your account, passport, photos, postcards, friends and shared trips from our servers and from your device.
  • “Reset my passport” clears everything stored on your device.

Children

Postmark isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
