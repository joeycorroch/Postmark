# Postmark art style guide

Every illustration in Postmark (Pip, the Postie Balloon, the map animals, the fishing boat, avatars) shares one look. Use this guide for anything new so it matches.

## The look
- Cute, chunky "sticker" cartoon for young adults: friendly, never babyish.
- Thick dark navy outline (#222B48) around every shape, with rounded line ends.
- Flat colour with one soft shade on the underside and one small white shine on top. No textures, no realistic fur or photo detail.
- Big round dark eyes with one or two white shine dots, small rosy cheeks, small happy smile.
- Soft, rounded proportions: big head, short legs, chunky body.
- Each subject stands on (or in) a small rounded base showing its home: sand, ice, grass, water, rock.
- Palette leans on the app colours: mint #00C56E, charcoal #1E2B33, yellow #FFC531, coral #FF6B5B, sky #8EDCEB, navy #2C3A5C, plus natural colours for the animal itself.

## Sheet rules (so pictures cut out cleanly)
- Plain light blue background (#B4D6F4), nothing else behind the subjects.
- Each subject inside a white sticker border, with clear space between stickers.
- Name label in bold navy capitals **under** each sticker, not touching it.
- No white parts touching the white border (white spouts, snow and smoke get lost when the background is removed). Give white details a navy outline and a hint of pale blue.
- Square or 16:9 image, at least 2000 px wide; 12 subjects per sheet in a 4 x 3 grid.

## Prompt to paste into an image generator
> A sticker sheet of 12 cute cartoon [SUBJECTS], arranged in a 4 by 3 grid on a plain light blue background. Chunky kawaii style for young adults, thick dark navy outlines, flat colours with a soft shade and a small white shine, big round dark eyes with white highlights, rosy cheeks, small smiles. Each subject stands on a small rounded base showing where it lives, and sits inside its own white sticker border with space around it. Bold navy capital name label under each sticker. White details like snow, water spray or smoke have a navy outline and a pale blue tint. No background scenery, no text other than the labels.
